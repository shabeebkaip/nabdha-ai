The Market Problem

Having the data is not the same as benefiting from it.

Businesses rely on data spread across sales, inventory, customers, expenses, payments, orders and delivery. Business owners often struggle to know what is actually happening, why it is happening, what might happen next, and what to do about it.

What's actually happening?

Data lives scattered across systems — the owner rarely has one clear picture of the business's real state.

Dashboards show numbers, not decisions

Traditional reporting tools display what happened — they stop short of explaining why, or what to do next.

Problems are found too late

Without continuous analysis, risks like stockouts, cash flow pressure, or customer loss surface only after they've already cost money.

The Core Advantage

Decision Intelligence, not just reporting.

Nabdha differs from traditional reporting tools, which mainly display data and explain what happened. It goes further — into why it happened, what could happen next, and what to do.

Code calculates. AI explains. Every number in a Nabdha report — growth rate, stock-out date, cash forecast — is produced by code running directly on the owner's data. Examples of Risks Nabdha Can Detect

Early warning, before it costs money.

Stockouts

A fast-selling product may run out before the next supply arrives.

Overstock

Slow-selling products with high stock levels tying up capital.

Declining Sales

Sales of a product, branch or business line steadily declining.

Declining Profits

Good sales, but falling profits from rising costs or shrinking margins.

Rising Expenses

Expenses rising faster than revenue.

Cash Flow Problems

Incoming cash may not cover upcoming payments and expenses.

Customer Loss

Regular customers who have started to stop buying.

Lower Purchase Frequency

Customers returning to buy less often than before.

Rising Delivery Costs

Rising delivery cost eating into order profitability.

Declining Product Performance

A product that used to sell well losing demand.

Single-Product Reliance

A large share of revenue depending on one product.

Customer Concentration

A large share of revenue from a small number of customers.

Slowing Growth

Sales or customer growth slowing versus previous periods.

Delivery Problems

Rising delivery time or order costs affecting satisfaction.

Abnormal Change

A sudden, unusual change in sales, expenses or orders worth checking.

A Simplified Real-World Example

One store's data — one risk, one opportunity, one plan.

Suppose Nabdha analyzed an online store's data and discovered the following.

🟢 The Opportunity

A Simplified Real-World Example

From signal to a tracked, measured plan.

Suggested Action Plan

1. Address the Risk — Product A

  • ✓Review current stock
  • ✓Place an urgent supply order
  • ✓Set a safety stock level to avoid recurrence
  • ✓Track sales daily until supply arrives

2. Seize the Opportunity — Product B

  • ✓Increase availability of Product B
  • ✓Increase its visibility in marketing campaigns
  • ✓Test a bundle offer combining Product B with Product C
  • ✓Track the offer's results and sales rate

Follow-Up & Impact Measurement

  • ✓Monitor stock levels
  • ✓Track sales of Product B and Product C
  • ✓Compare results before and after the actions are taken
  • ✓Update recommendations based on the new data
Expected Outcome: Reduced risk of losing sales due to Product A running out, while capitalizing on rising demand for Product B to increase sales and take advantage of the cross-selling opportunity.

Business Model & Revenue Streams

Eight streams, one revenue ecosystem.

1. SaaS Subscriptions

Monthly and annual plans — the platform's core, recurring revenue.

2. Premium AI Reports

An AI Business Health Report — performance, top risks, growth opportunities, customer & product analysis, cash flow indicators, recommendations — sold separately or bundled into higher tiers.

3. AI Business Review

Periodic (monthly / quarterly) review service with a full action plan.

4. Premium Integrations

Advanced connections to accounting, POS, e-commerce, payment and inventory systems.

5. Enterprise Solutions

Custom solutions for larger businesses — more users, integrations, permissions, dedicated support.

6. Consulting & Expert Services

Connects owners to consultants, financial advisors, accountants and marketing experts — Nabdha earns commission.

7. Training & Education

Courses on data-driven decisions, AI for SMEs, financial and inventory management.

8. Digital Business Products

Templates and toolkits — cash flow, KPI, financial planning, business growth toolkits.

Open Questions

What's worth resolving before the financial proposal.

Drawn directly from gaps and ambiguities already flagged in Nabdha's own brief — not new scope, just decisions worth making explicit.

Product & Packaging
  • ●Align the onboarding package names (Free / Premium / Pro) with the pricing plan tiers (Basic / Growth / Pro / Enterprise) into one scheme
  • ●Confirm final pricing once market testing is complete — current figures are explicitly preliminary
  • ●Define exactly what the Free tier includes, versus what requires Premium/Pro
Data & Integrations
  • ●Prioritize which system integration ships first once MVP upload is live — order matters for which SME segment onboards next
  • ●Confirm which external AI/LLM service powers the analysis and Smart Business Advisor, and its data-handling terms

Open Questions

Market & Language
  • ●Confirm initial target industries/sectors within the SME segment, and whether launch is Saudi-only or wider GCC/Arab market

Business Model
